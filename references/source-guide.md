# Choose a repository by the question

These are starting points. Cite the particular schedule, newspaper page or register you actually read. Coverage, access and download rights vary by title, year and holding institution.

| When you need to know … | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Who lived in a U.S. household, and how did the enumerator describe them? | Use the National Archives' census guide to find the schedule for the right year. Record enumeration district, sheet, line and household. Look on the next sheet when a household runs to the bottom. [U.S. National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/research/census) |
| Which court or parish kept the register? | Search the FamilySearch Catalog by the place as it was known at the time. Read the catalogue description, years, film or digital group number and image-access conditions. A series may have images without a name index. For European holdings, a finding aid can identify the responsible archive and the record series in the local language. [FamilySearch Catalog](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog) · [Archives Portal Europe](https://www.archivesportaleurope.net/) |
| Where might a small-town account survive? | Check Chronicling America for digitized U.S. titles and dates. Also check the state newspaper project or local library. Newspapers.com can fill gaps when the researcher has authorized access. Browse neighboring issues or towns once you know the date. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/collections/chronicling-america/about-this-collection/) · [Newspapers.com](https://www.newspapers.com/) |
| Where can I find a directory, yearbook or association's proceedings? | Try the institution that issued it, then look up its contemporary title and year in HathiTrust or Internet Archive. Compare the title page and volume number before substituting another copy. Full-text availability can differ. [HathiTrust Digital Library](https://www.hathitrust.org/) · [Internet Archive texts](https://archive.org/details/texts) |
| Did the family move or did the U.S. county boundary change? | Check the county chronology at the relevant date. Use it to decide where to seek the next deed, tax list or probate proceeding. For another country use its equivalent boundary history or gazetteer. [Newberry Library’s Atlas of Historical County Boundaries](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/) |
