# Family research casebook

## Research agreement

Starting person and anchor record · priority branch · relationship to test · life detail to investigate · agreed sources, contact and access limits · intended audience and treatment of living people · requested deliverable. Record changes of scope with their date.

## Starting account

| Person ID | Name as given and variants | Date or range and place | What was remembered | Who supplied it and when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Source register

| Source ID | Creator, collection or publication, date and repository | Printed page, entry, image or lines | Stable URL and access date | Local original or permitted extract, derivative and reuse limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Claims and relationships

| Person IDs | Claim or relationship, including direction | Source IDs and page locators | What the sources say and who said it | Confidence, conflict and next discriminating record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Life events and stories

| People | Event date and precision | Place and precision | Scene, action or short attributed quotation | Source ID, locator and report date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Search log

For a team search, put the assigned researcher beside each open question and link their handoff in the outcome column. Give each pending download one retry owner in the shared queue below.

| Question | Collection, jurisdiction and actual coverage | Names, variants or pages checked | Outcome, search date and next lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Shared retrieval queue

One entry per outstanding artifact. Point all branch researchers here. If the project already has a queue, link to it here instead.

| Source or item | Stable locator and desired page | Why it matters | Access condition, last attempt and result | Next permitted attempt or item for researcher | Verified local copy when recovered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Handoff

Summarize the best-supported relationship and one new detail of a person's life. Name the first unproven link and the next action likely to resolve it. Link the family-facing result and its saved evidence.
